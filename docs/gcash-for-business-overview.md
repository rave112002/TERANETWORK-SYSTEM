# GCash for Business: Key Overview & Features

GCash for Business (G4B) is a comprehensive suite of digital financial solutions and merchant tools designed by GCash (operated by Mynt / Globe Fintech Innovations) to help micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), as well as large corporations, streamline payment collection, disbursement, and financial management.

---

## 1. Key Features & Solutions

GCash for Business covers several primary functional areas tailored to business size and operational requirements:

### A. Payment Acceptance (Accept Payments Anywhere)
* **Digital QR / Merchant QR:** Static or dynamic QR PH codes for physical storefronts or online transactions. Customers scan to pay directly from their GCash wallet.
* **POS Devices:** Dedicated wireless smart Android point-of-sale terminals designed to accept QR payments, card payments (Visa/Mastercard), and print physical transaction receipts.
* **Online Payment Gateways & APIs:** E-commerce integration (via GCash Web Pay, checkout plugins like Shopify/WooCommerce, or custom API integration) allowing customers to pay online securely.
* **GCash Pera Drop / Cash-In Outlets:** Services enabling physical branch locations to serve as cash-in and cash-out points for the public.

### B. Financial Management & Portal
* **GCash for Business Merchant Portal:** A web-based dashboard for real-time monitoring of transactions, available balances, branch sales, and revenue tracking.
* **Multi-User Access & Role Management:** Business owners can grant restricted access to store managers or cashiers without giving full control over the main account or wallet withdrawals.
* **Automated Settlement & Transfers:** Automated bank transfers and payouts directly into a linked corporate or personal bank account.
* **Downloadable Financial Reports:** Automated generation of PDF/CSV transaction logs for easy reconciliation, tax filing, and auditing.

### C. Disbursements & Payroll (G4B Enterprise/Pro)
* **Bulk Disbursements:** Direct mass payouts to employees, contractual workers, or suppliers directly into their GCash wallets (ideal for payroll, incentives, or loan disbursements).
* **Corporate Loans & Financing:** Access to institutional business credit line options (e.g., Fuse Lending/GCredit solutions) designed specifically for merchants based on transaction volume.

---

## 2. Main Benefits for Business Owners

* **Bypasses Standard Wallet Limits:** Unlike personal GCash accounts (which cap monthly incoming/outgoing limits), merchant business accounts offer significantly higher or custom limits tailored to actual operational volume.
* **Higher Customer Conversion:** Integrates with the Philippines' largest e-wallet ecosystem, reducing abandoned online carts and lost storefront sales.
* **QR PH Compatibility:** Fully compatible with standard Philippine QR PH specifications, enabling payments from other banks (e.g., Maya, BDO, BPI) directly into the GCash merchant account.
* **Cash Handling Reduction:** Minimizes cash storage risks, theft, counterfeit bills, and the need for physical loose change.

---

## 3. Merchant Rates & Fees (MDR)

GCash operates on a **Merchant Discount Rate (MDR)** model for business transactions:

* **Standard In-Store QR Payments:** Typically around **1.5% to 2%** per transaction (varies by tier and promo programs).
* **Online Payment Gateways:** Varies between **2.0% and 3.5%** depending on payment integration type and volume.
* **Settlement Fees:** Automated payouts to linked partner bank accounts are typically free or offered at nominal rates depending on agreement terms.
